# NLP Lab - Assignment 01

## Overview
Explores corpus analysis, morphological transformations, spelling error correction with edit distance, and statistical N-gram language modeling using the NLTK Brown Corpus.

## Notebooks
- **[Task_1.ipynb](file:///home/parth/Lab/NLP/Assignment_01/notebooks/Task_1.ipynb)**: Brown Corpus Preprocessing & Lexical Normalization
  - Sentence segmentation, stopword filtering, punctuation removal, and vocabulary diversity analysis.
- **[Task_2.ipynb](file:///home/parth/Lab/NLP/Assignment_01/notebooks/Task_2.ipynb)**: Stemming vs. Lemmatization Comparison
  - Comparative analysis of Porter Stemming vs. WordNet Lemmatization on Brown vocabulary and benchmark targets (`playing`, `studies`, `running`, `better`, `cars`).
- **[Task_3.ipynb](file:///home/parth/Lab/NLP/Assignment_01/notebooks/Task_3.ipynb)**: Edit Distance Spell Checking
  - Minimum Edit Distance (Levenshtein) correction engine evaluated on 10 misspelled benchmark words and full noisy sentences.
- **[Task_4.ipynb](file:///home/parth/Lab/NLP/Assignment_01/notebooks/Task_4.ipynb)**: N-gram Language Modeling
  - Generation and frequency distribution analysis of Unigrams, Bigrams, and Trigrams from the Brown Corpus.
